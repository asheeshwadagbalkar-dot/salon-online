Salon Owners Ki Roz Ki Tension

Kya Aapke Salon Mein Bhi Ye Problems Hain?

Salon chalana sirf haircuts, styling aur beauty services tak limited nahi hai — jab operations manual hote hain toh ye 6 dikkatein roz aati hain:

01

Appointment miss ho jate hain

Diary ya phone calls par likhi bookings peak hours mein overlap ya miss ho jati hain.

02

Billing aur daily sales tracking confusing hai

Paper bills aur manual calculation mein cash, UPI, card aur stylist-wise total match karna mushkil hota hai.

03

Customer history scattered hai

Client ke pichle visits, loyalty points, wallet balance aur service preferences ek jagah nahi milte.

04

Staff schedules manage karna mushkil hai

Kaunsa stylist ya beautician kis shift mein hai aur kis client par assigned hai, iska clear view nahi rehta.

05

Products ka stock time par pata nahi chalta

Shampoo, hair color aur facial kits ka current stock aur reorder point bina manual checking ke pata nahi chalta.

06

Repeat customer follow-up manual hai

Clients ko reminders, greetings aur promotional coupons bhejne mein bahut waqt lagta hai.

FAQs

Aksar Puche Jaane Wale Sawaal

1. Kya Salon OS beauty parlour aur unisex salon ke liye hai? +

Haan, Salon OS Indian beauty parlours, hair salons, unisex salons, spas aur salon chains ke daily workflows ke hisaab se banaya gaya hai. Aap apni service categories (Hair, Skin & Facials, Nail Art, Bridal, Men's Grooming) apne salon ke mutabik set kar sakte hain.

2. Kya appointment aur billing ek jagah manage kar sakte hain? +

Ji haan. Dashboard mein Appointments & Calendar aur Live POS Terminal (Billing) dono ek hi jagah available hain, jisse aap bookings aur daily sales billing aasani se manage kar sakte hain.

3. Kya customer records maintain ho sakte hain? +

Haan, Customer CRM section mein aap client profiles, phone numbers, visit counts, loyalty points aur wallet balances ko organized rakh sakte hain.

4. Kya staff aur inventory management available hai? +

Haan, Staff & Roster mein aap team details aur weekly shifts manage kar sakte hain, aur Inventory & Products mein current stock aur reorder points review kar sakte hain.

5. Kya demo free hai? +

Haan, live dashboard walkthrough 100% free aur no-obligation hai.

6. Setup aur pricing kaise pata chalegi? +

Demo walkthrough ke dauran hamari team aapke salon ke workflow, branches aur required modules ko samajh kar aapko exact setup aur pricing details batayegi.

7. Kya WhatsApp features ke liye additional integration required hai? +

Haan. Dashboard mein WhatsApp Automation module diya gaya hai, lekin live customer reminders aur automated messages tabhi send hote hain jab aapke account mein WhatsApp integration/API configured aur enabled ho.
